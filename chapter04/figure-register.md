# Chapter 4 figure briefs

Chapter 4 contains no inserted figures. These are text-only briefs for possible later artwork. Each caption and description belongs in the relevant section source. Numbering remains provisional until the text is stable.

A description should explain what the reader would see and why it clarifies the argument. Keep it to a few sentences. Do not write an image-generation prompt, invent data, or imply that a paper's artwork may be reproduced without a separate rights check.

## Production boundary

- No files are stored under `images/chapter04/`.
- No Markdown image syntax or AsciiDoc `image::` macro is permitted in a Chapter 4 source.
- The assembled PDF must contain captions and descriptions only, with no figure artwork.
- Any later artwork requires an independent editorial pass, exact-source/license review and explicit instruction to begin figure production.
