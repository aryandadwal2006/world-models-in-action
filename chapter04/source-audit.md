## How to interpret this audit

The paper text was reviewed before substantive section revisions. The technical claims in the chapter are attributed to the exact versions listed in `references.md`. Figures, code licenses and paper licenses are treated separately: citing a paper does not grant reuse permission for its source figures or implementation.

The access limitation is specific and unresolved: **the exact eight PDF binaries stored in the repository have not been opened locally or visually checked page by page in this session.** The primary-source text review covers the paper content needed for the present draft, but it cannot rule out extraction omissions in figure labels, footnotes, tables or equations whose meaning depends on page layout. A literal page-by-page audit of the supplied binaries remains open until those files are available through a PDF-capable file interface.

## Source-specific checks that affected the manuscript

- **I-JEPA:** the target encoder processes the full image before selected target positions are read out. The context encoder receives the visible tokens. Mask positions are represented by positional queries to the predictor.
- **Original V-JEPA:** short-range and long-range multi-block masks are distinct parameterizations; spatial target blocks are extended across the clip. Causal-prefix masking is a separate ablation and must not be inferred from the default objective.
- **V-JEPA 2:** action-free video pretraining and robot action-conditioned post-training are distinct stages. DROID supplies visual trajectories and end-effector state; the transition predictor is trained with both teacher forcing and a short autoregressive rollout objective.
- **LeJEPA:** the Cramér–Wold theorem concerns all directions; the statistical test uses a maximum over a tested set, while the practical training regularizer averages directional statistics to distribute gradients. The published loss applies SIGReg separately to each view and averages those view losses.
- **VISReg:** scale, shape and centering are distinct regularization components. Shape matching uses sorted one-dimensional projections after standard-deviation normalization with a stop-gradient boundary. The paper's printed squared-norm formula and pseudocode's mean reduction differ by a batch-size normalization factor.
- **VL-JEPA:** the target can be textual rather than visual. The model predicts a continuous text embedding conditioned on visual input and a query; a decoder is used when surface text is required.
- **V-JEPA 2.1:** context loss supervises visible tokens with inverse-square-root weighting by their nearest masked-token distance; deep supervision uses three intermediate encoder blocks plus the final block. Robot planning still uses a separately trained action-conditioned predictor.
- **LeCun's proposal:** six top-level modules are distinguished from subcomponents within Cost; Mode 1 and Mode 2 and the hierarchy are presented as a research architecture, not a validated end-to-end system.
