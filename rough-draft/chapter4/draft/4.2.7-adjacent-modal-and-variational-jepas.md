# 4.2.7 Related JEPA directions: modalities and uncertainty

The image and video examples are the main progression of this chapter. Other JEPA papers extend the same predictive idea to additional modalities or change the mathematical treatment of uncertainty. They are important to recognize, but they do not all define one unified architecture.

## Audio prediction

A-JEPA (Fei, Fan and Huang, 2023) applies joint-embedding prediction to audio. Its inputs are represented through time-frequency structure, and its masking strategy accounts for the fact that time and frequency axes have different meanings. Audio-JEPA (Tuncay and colleagues, 2025) is a separate work with a separate method and evaluation. It uses masked audio representations and evaluates learned features on audio tasks. A-JEPA and Audio-JEPA should not be merged because the names are similar.

The general design choice is the same as in image and video JEPA: select a context, define a target representation, and predict the target in the embedding space. But the target geometry changes. A time-frequency patch is not interchangeable with an image patch, and the mask should reflect the structure of the signal.

## Vision-language prediction

VL-JEPA (Chen and colleagues, ICLR 2026) predicts continuous embeddings of target text rather than autoregressively generating token sequences during its main predictive training. The model is trained to align visual context with a representation of language. A text decoder is used when a textual answer must be produced. The paper evaluates retrieval, open-vocabulary recognition and visual question answering.

This is a useful demonstration that the target embedding need not be visual. It is not a guarantee that a visual-language embedding contains the physical state needed for manipulation. A language-aligned representation is optimized for cross-modal tasks; control sufficiency remains a separate requirement.

## Variational and probabilistic formulations

Two 2026 works use related but distinct names and should be differentiated carefully.

**Var-JEPA** (Gögl and Yau, ICML 2026) derives a variational formulation by making an underlying coupled latent-variable structure explicit. It introduces an evidence lower bound (ELBO) and studies uncertainty-aware latent prediction. The paper instantiates the approach for tabular data through Var-T-JEPA. Its central claim is that a deterministic JEPA can be understood as a special case of a more explicit variational construction under the paper's model class.

**VJEPA** (Huang, ICML 2026) is a different paper. It proposes probabilistic prediction distributions over future latent states and connects the model to predictive state representations and Bayesian filtering. The paper also introduces Bayesian JEPA, which combines learned dynamics with modular prior experts through a Product of Experts. It evaluates uncertainty-aware predictions in several benchmark settings, including continuous-control tasks.

The names are easy to confuse. Var-JEPA and VJEPA are separate ICML papers with different authors, methods and empirical scope. Neither should be described as merely a new version of V-JEPA for video. In this chapter, the deterministic JEPA examples are sufficient to develop the main architecture; the variational papers provide a compact pointer toward probabilistic latent prediction.

## A compact map of the family

| Direction | Target representation | Distinctive emphasis |
|---|---|---|
| I-JEPA | Hidden image regions | Spatial context-to-target prediction |
| V-JEPA | Masked video regions | Spatiotemporal feature prediction |
| V-JEPA 2 | Video features; later robot-conditioned future features | Action-free pretraining followed by action-conditioned post-training |
| V-JEPA 2.1 | Dense image/video token features | Spatially structured and temporally consistent representations |
| A-JEPA | Audio time-frequency features | Audio-aware context and masking |
| Audio-JEPA | Audio features | A distinct audio JEPA formulation and evaluation |
| VL-JEPA | Text embeddings conditioned on vision | Vision-language prediction in embedding space |
| Var-JEPA | Latent-variable predictive representation | Variational derivation and ELBO |
| VJEPA | Distribution over future latent states | Probabilistic world models and filtering |
